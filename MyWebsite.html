CHINESE CUISINE

Welcome to Chinese Cuisine

Chinese cuisine is one of the world's oldest and most diverse culinary traditions. Each region has its own cooking style, ingredients, and flavors.


Major Regional Cuisines

  • Cantonese – Famous for Dim Sum and Roast Duck.
  • Sichuan – Known for spicy dishes and Sichuan pepper.
  • Hunan – Hot and smoky flavors.
  • Jiangsu – Delicate seafood dishes.
  • Zhejiang – Fresh seasonal ingredients.
  • Fujian – Rich soups and seafood.
  • Anhui – Wild herbs and mountain cuisine.
  • Shandong – Wheat foods and seafood.
